# 💫 About Me:
Hi, I'm Kevin — AI engineer, founder, and someone still trying to prove that AI can actually help people learn... and not just do the learning for them.<br><br>🚀 **Currently building**:<br>- **[Scholera](https://scholera.com)** — *Scholars, not scores.* An AI-native layer for higher education that plugs into Canvas, Blackboard, Moodle, and Brightspace. RAG-powered course-specific tutoring, living roadmaps, an adaptive quiz studio, and co-pilot grading. Live at multiple US universities.<br>- Backed by **$100K** from New Jersey's $3.34M AI education grant pool (selected from 280+ teams) · **NJ Plug and Play & NJEADA Fellow**.<br>- **MCP servers & agent tooling** — real-time intent classification exposed as tools, sub-second inference, and the unglamorous tenant-isolation work nobody posts about.<br><br>🧠 **Learning & exploring**:<br>- Reinforcement learning for adaptive curricula (DQN + Bayesian Knowledge Tracing, Elo-based skill estimation)<br>- Agent infrastructure — MCP, tool layers, prompt caching, and latency budgets that actually hold in production<br>- Deep learning for audio — transformer denoisers, psychoacoustic masking, streaming inference<br><br>📄 **Published**: *WaveSplit: A Multi-Stage Framework for Audio Enhancement and Audio Denoising* — beat the NVIDIA CleanUNet baseline by +2.3 dB SNR and +0.21 PESQ under non-stationary noise.<br><br>🎓 **Also**: MS in Computer Science @ Stevens Institute of Technology (4.0 GPA) · 5x hackathon winner · open source contributor to **pandas**, **scipy**, and **scikit-learn**.<br><br>📘 **Fun fact**: I'm deeply curious about how technology can improve inner clarity, mental well-being, and lifelong learning — one model at a time.<br><br>---<br><br>Thanks for visiting my profile!<br>
