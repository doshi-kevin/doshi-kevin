# 💫 About Me:
Hi, I'm Kevin — an aspiring AI engineer trying to prove that AI can actually help people get jobs... and not just take them all first.<br><br>🎧 **Currently building**:<br>- A voice denoising system using CleanUNet and UrbanSound8K, aimed at isolating human voice from real-world noise.<br>- Noise-specific denoisers with voice activity–aware loss reweighting and time-frequency mask supervision.<br>- A personal AI project called **Aura.AI** – a self-development assistant that learns from the wisdom of popular books and provides bite-sized, actionable guidance.<br><br>🧠 **Learning & exploring**:<br>- Deep learning for audio (PyTorch, UNet variants)<br>- Voice activity detection (VAD) and real-time denoising techniques<br>- NLP pipelines to train models on self-help literature<br><br>📘 **Fun fact**: I’m deeply curious about how technology can improve inner clarity, mental well-being, and lifelong learning — one model at a time.<br><br>---<br><br>Thanks for visiting my profile!<br>
